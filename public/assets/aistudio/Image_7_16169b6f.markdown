## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the contemplative, tactile sophistication of high-end independent publishing, drawing direct aesthetic lineage from periodicals like *Kinfolk*, *Cereal*, and *Architectural Digest*. The interface bridges physical print permanence with the fluidity of an interactive platform, centered on intentional living across three holistic pillars: Health & Wellness, Sustainable Wealth, and Family Life.

### Design Principles
- **Editorial Intentionality:** Every element on the page exists for a reason. Generous, unhurried negative space frames content as curated artifacts rather than infinite feeds.
- **Organic Restraint:** Natural, tactile tones supplant clinical neutrals. Surfaces evoke uncoated archival paper, muted botanicals, and warm sunlit stone.
- **Architectural Harmony:** Visual hierarchy relies on classical typographical proportions, disciplined hairline dividers, and subdued planar shifts rather than aggressive shadows or neon flourishes.
- **Tactile Comfort:** Interactions mimic physical page dynamics—quiet micro-transitions, subtle focus rings, and soft, deliberate state changes that evoke a relaxed reading environment.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an organic editorial palette that balances botanical vitality with grounded permanence. 

- **Primary (`#8BA58D` - Sage Green):** The foundational pillar tone representing Health & Wellness. Used for interactive highlights, secondary active indicators, editorial badges, and atmospheric hero accents.
- **Secondary (`#234B3B` - Deep Forest Green):** The authoritative grounding anchor representing longevity and Sustainable Wealth. Applied to primary call-to-actions, high-priority navigation links, dark hero surfaces, and prominent display typography.
- **Tertiary (`#D9C5A1` - Muted Champagne):** A soft, tactile gold that brings editorial warmth to feature callouts, pillar badges, and subtle highlight backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#74776F` - Warm Grey):** A mid-toned stone neutral for secondary body copy, captions, and structural metadata.
- **Canvas & Surface Backgrounds:** The base canvas operates on warm ivory (`#FAF8F3`), transitioning to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated reading cards and focus panels, bounded by soft neutral dividers (`#E8E5DD`) and anchored with deep charcoal (`#252923`) primary text.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical humanism with contemporary functional clarity. 

- **Headlines (EB Garamond):** Used exclusively for editorial titles, drop caps, section introductions, and blockquotes. Its calligraphic weight and proportions introduce an intellectual, romantic cadence. All display headers utilize tight, purposeful line height paired with gentle negative tracking to emulate fine hot-metal typesetting.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Chosen for its humanist geometry and soft legibility. Long-form article copy maintains a relaxed 1.6 to 1.7 line-height ratio to prevent visual fatigue.
- **Labels & Overlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Small metadata tags, pillar labels, and system status indicators are set in uppercase, semi-bold weights with wide letter spacing (+0.06em to +0.08em) to create clear editorial classification.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors print magazine column grids, using a responsive 12-column structure on desktop that collapses to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Editorial Margin Ratios:** Canvas edges maintain generous exterior boundaries (`margin: 4rem` on desktop) to enclose articles like open spreads. 
- **Column Dynamics:** Long-form reading containers are capped at an optimal reading measure (maximum 680px width, spanning 8 central columns or 7 offset columns) flanked by asymmetric white space or marginalia for pull-quotes and footnotes.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** A base grid of 8px drives vertical increments. Distinct articles and pillar sections are partitioned with breathing room (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) rather than heavy physical dividing walls, preserving an uncluttered environment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through planar surface shifts and quiet tactile layering rather than dramatic drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary level sits on `#FAF8F3` (Warm Ivory). Focused content, modular cards, and reading panels transition to pure `#FFFFFF`. Overlay menus and popovers sit on `#FFFFFF` bordered with hairline boundaries.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Spatial boundaries rely on 1px solid borders in `#E8E5DD`. When separation is needed between stacked editorial modules, thin rules mimic the layout guidelines of print periodicals.
- **Diffuse Ambient Shadows:** Elevated layers (such as floating reading navigation, search overlays, and interactive dialogs) employ an ethereal, low-contrast shadow tinted with warm earthy undertones (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(35, 75, 59, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(37, 41, 35, 0.03)`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, tailored shape language defined by a subtle roundedness level (`roundedness: 1`). 

- **Base Curvature (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to form elements, standard button containers, badges, and inline UI items. This soft rounding takes the sharp edge off rigid geometry while preserving an editorial, paper-cut silhouette.
- **Container Curvature (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Reserved for media containers, featured article cards, and modal dialogs.
- **Curvature Hierarchy (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px):** Applied exclusively to floating interactive panels and persistent bottom drawers on mobile devices.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Pillar badges and category micro-tags may utilize full pill radii (`rounded-full`) when combined with low-saturation pastel tints to communicate category ownership.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Forest Green (`#234B3B`) with white text, 0.25rem border-radius, generous horizontal padding (`space-lg`), and subtle hover transition to an organic forest-olive mix (`#1B3B2E`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base with 1px border in `#234B3B` and matching text. Hover states introduce a warm tint fill (`rgba(139, 165, 141, 0.08)`).
- **Text Link:** EB Garamond or Plus Jakarta Sans with a persistent hairline underline in `#8BA58D`, offset by 4px, transitioning to solid color on interaction.

### Chips & Pillar Badges
- Small-scale metadata containers set in `label-sm` with letter spacing.
- **Health & Wellness:** Background `rgba(139, 165, 141, 0.15)` with text `#234B3B`.
- **Finance:** Background `rgba(217, 197, 161, 0.25)` with text `#594928`.
- **Family:** Background `rgba(209, 134, 114, 0.15)` with text `#6E392B`.
- Outlines remain subtle (0.5px or none) to maintain an organic, printed appearance.

### Cards & Editorial Modules
- **Featured Story Card:** Clean `#FFFFFF` surface with hairline border (`#E8E5DD`), full-bleed or inset photography with a 3:2 or 4:5 aspect ratio, EB Garamond title, and discrete category tag.
- **Minimal List Item:** Borderless layout separated solely by bottom horizontal rules (`1px solid #E8E5DD`), displaying article sequence number, title, and estimated reading duration.

### Input Fields & Forms
- Minimalist bounding with 1px `#E8E5DD` borders on `#FFFFFF` backgrounds.
- Focus state replaces the border with `#8BA58D` and an ethereal `0 0 0 2px rgba(139, 165, 141, 0.2)` ring.
- Placeholder text in `#74776F` at 70% opacity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes utilize soft corners (2px radius); radio buttons remain circular.
- Unselected: 1.5px border `#E8E5DD` on white background.
- Selected: Solid `#234B3B` fill with an off-white inner check or center dot.

### Specialized Editorial Components
- **Drop Caps:** EB Garamond display initial spanning 3 lines of body text, tinted in `#234B3B` or `#8BA58D`.
- **Pull Quotes:** Centered or margin-aligned, set in `headline-md` italic, accompanied by top and bottom hairline borders in `#D9C5A1`.
- **Reading Progress Bar:** A discreet 2px high indicator anchored to the viewport header, filled with `#8BA58D`.
